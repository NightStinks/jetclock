JetClock

v1.0.0

Flight tracking for your wall

JetClock turns a £16 touchscreen into a live flight radar and smart home controller. No ESPHome. No YAML. Just plug in and flash.

Flash firmware

Connect your Guition ESP32-S3-4848S040 via USB-C, then click the button below.
Chrome or Edge required — Web Serial is not supported in Firefox or Safari.

Requires Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS, or Linux

After flashing
1

Connect to the setup network

The display creates a Wi-Fi access point called JetClock-Setup. Connect to it from your phone or laptop — a setup page will open automatically.

2

Fill in your details

Enter your home Wi-Fi, your latitude/longitude, and your Home Assistant URL and token. Pick up to 5 HA entities for the smart home buttons.

3

Save & done

Hit Save & reboot. The display restarts, connects to your network, and starts showing flights overhead within 30 seconds.

🖥

Guition ESP32-S3-4848S040

480×480 touchscreen display, ESP32-S3 dual-core, 16 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM. Available on AliExpress for around £16. This is the only supported hardware for v1.
